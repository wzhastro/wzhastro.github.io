About Me

I am an Associate Professor at the School of Mathematics and Information Sciences at Yantai University. My research focuses on astronomy and astrophysics, with a particular emphasis on eclipsing binary systems. By leveraging large-scale astronomical survey data (Kepler, TESS, LAMOST, Gaia, etc.) combined with applied statistics and machine learning methods, I investigate the physical properties, orbital evolution, and population statistics of binary and planetary systems.
